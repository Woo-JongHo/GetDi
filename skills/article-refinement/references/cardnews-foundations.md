# Cardnews foundations

Use these as evidence-backed starting points, not universal guarantees.

| ID | Principle | GetDi application | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| FMT-01 | Instagram preserves supported photos up to 1080px wide. | Use 1080×1350px as the project’s 4:5 portrait canvas. | [Instagram Help Centre](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/1631821640426723) |
| COPY-01 | Screen content should be concise, scannable, and use meaningful headings. | Use one title, one core sentence, and only necessary supporting copy. | [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/be-succinct-writing-for-the-web/) |
| SEG-01 | Complex material can be easier to process when divided into learner-paced segments. | Put one primary claim on each card. This is a derived application. | [Mayer & Moreno](https://www.psychology.mcmaster.ca/bennett/psy720/readings/m1/m1r3.pdf) |
| IMG-01 | Extraneous words and pictures can impair understanding. | Prefer article-body images that explain the current claim; omit decorative media. | [Mayer & Moreno](https://www.psychology.mcmaster.ca/bennett/psy720/readings/m1/m1r3.pdf) |
| REL-01 | Corresponding words and images should be spatially close. | Put labels and explanations next to the relevant image region. | [Mayer & Moreno](https://www.psychology.mcmaster.ca/bennett/psy720/readings/m1/m1r3.pdf) |
| A11Y-01 | WCAG minimum contrast is 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. | Validate text/background pairs before export. | [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum) |

## Project decisions, not research findings

- Default six-card flow: cover → context → three insights → source/close
- File size target: 1.2MB or less
- Prefer original article-body media over listing thumbnails
- Preserve a direct route from every factual card claim to its source block
- Cover: 50pt subtitle + 100pt title, maximum two or three lines, background image with black gradient
- Body: fixed top signature `네카라쿠배 디자이너, 피그마스터`
- Body: 80pt title limited to one or two lines + 45pt body copy
- Body: place the relevant image in the middle or bottom area
